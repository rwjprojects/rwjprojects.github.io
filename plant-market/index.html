What the words mean

Reference. Anything on this page that sounds like jargon, explained.

Money taken / taken so far
What a live show has actually sold, in dollars, while it is still running. The platform publishes it. (An analyst would call this GMV — gross merchandise value — but it just means total sales.)
Median
The middle value. Half are above, half below. Used instead of an average because one $2,000 plant would drag an average somewhere misleading.
Landed price
What the buyer actually pays: the plant plus shipping. It matters more than you would think — shipping runs a median 63% of the item price here.
Spread
The gap between the cheapest and dearest seller of the same plant. A big spread means the market has not settled on a price, which is where room usually is.
Top seller %
How much of a search term one single seller holds. Over about 25% and they own it; you would be fighting for scraps.
Named cultivar
A listing naming a specific variety, not just a genus — "Anthurium warocqueanum" rather than "anthurium". Used to check whether two platforms are really selling the same thing before calling a price gap arbitrage.
Confirmed / Likely / Weak
How much to trust a finding. Confirmed means a second independent source agreed — the other platform, or another day. Likely is one clear source. Weak is shown so you can look, not so you can rely on it.
Reviews per day
How fast a shop is transacting. Only a buyer can leave a review, so it is the closest thing to a sales rate either platform gives away — and unlike a total, it compares an old shop to a new one fairly.
